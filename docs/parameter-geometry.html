Understanding the values

A 0–1 proximity value follows a matching object as it moves closer. Close=1 marks entry into its contact region. X/Y/Z locate a target relative to its reference. Colored spheres or capsules show detection shapes; gray outlines identify the Socket, Plug and direction.
