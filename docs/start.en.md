# Guides

[简体中文](start.md) | **English**

Use VRChat/SPS, a funscript or manual controls to drive an OSR or another interactive device. Start with one connected device, then build the response you want.

## First connection

- [Follow the first-run walkthrough](quick-start.en.md) — choose your goal, connect one device and make your first movement.
- [Find your device connection](devices.en.md) — OSR/TCode, Intiface and other supported outputs, with desktop and browser choices.
- [Try WebSensa](websensa.en.md) — manual controls and funscripts in your browser, without installing Sensa.

## Continue with your goal

- [Use VRChat / SPS / OGB](usage-scenarios.en.md#existing-ogb--sps) — receive contact parameters, choose a mapping preset and calibrate its response.
- [Play a funscript or use sliders](websensa.en.md#manual-controls-and-scripts) — choose the input and the axis your device uses.
- [Connect a website or another player](protocol-inputs.en.md) — let desktop Sensa receive its controls and send them to your device.
- [Record an interaction or combine inputs](signal-pipeline.en.md) — save funscript tracks, smooth a signal or send it to several outputs.

## Devices and connections

- [OSR and TCode](devices.en.md#osr-and-tcode) — connect serial or network motion controllers and set their travel.
- [Intiface devices](devices.en.md#intiface-devices) — discover a device and select its position, vibration or rotation features.
- [Choose desktop or WebSensa](devices.en.md#choose-desktop-or-websensa) — check which host supports the connection you need.

## Look up a setting

- [Workbench map](guide.en.md) — find controls, presets, mappings and recordings.
- [Feature reference](features.en.md) — explore inputs, outputs, recordings and download formats.
- [Mapping presets](presets.en.md) and [OSC parameters](osc-parameters.en.md) — understand the values used by your Avatar.
- [Avatar tracking routes](usage-scenarios.en.md#vrchat-parameters-and-tracking) and [Unity package](../unity/top.sealoong.sensa/README.md) — add tracking when existing contact parameters are not enough.
- [Development and releases](development.en.md) — build, verify or extend Sensa.
