# Root measurement contracts and explicit spatial permission / 根点测量契约与明确空间许可

四点定位使用无版本路径中的 P0–P3 与合并有效性／采样活动的 Sample；Unity／桌面共享唯一几何，删除旧八路、移动参考、Others 和协议别名，减少错误组合与重复常量字段。静止距离允许缓存，并以内容变化的 Sample 判断生产端活动；这是对 delta-only 实用性的取舍，不证明 Contact 同帧或无丢包，内部缩放门禁仍必须保留。Shader 用独立原生命名空间、同来源完整五字段与变化 Frame，诊断另行隔离；独立 Point／Handle／Pose 保留各自时效，所有空间映射仍需实验许可与输入启用，失效即停止，任何 CRC、ID 或残差都不是所有者认证或世界真值。[参数与限制](../osc-parameters.md)

Four-anchor tracking uses unversioned P0–P3 and Sample combining validity with sampling activity. Unity/desktop share one geometry; old eight-range, moving-reference, Others and alias branches are removed to avoid mixed models and repeated constants. Stationary ranges may be cached while changing Sample proves producer activity, trading atomicity claims for usable delta-only input; internal scale guards remain essential. Shader uses a separate native namespace, five complete same-source fields and a changing Frame, with diagnostics isolated; independent Point/Handle/Pose retain their own age rules, and every spatial mapping still requires explicit experimental/input permission and stops on invalidity—CRC, identity and residual are neither owner authentication nor world truth. ## Coverage and image alignment / 覆盖范围与画面对齐

四点定位的接收半径为 2，测量点沿各坐标轴偏移 ±0.35，点间距约 0.990，均按 AvatarRootLocal 的制作局部单位定义。只有制作时与运行时的 Avatar 根缩放均为 1，这些数值才直接等于世界米。SDK 的 3 m 世界半径上限仍限制可用根缩放。四点有效区域须同时满足全部距离测量、未饱和与解算边界；覆盖显示按四个接收器的共同有效区域绘制。四点定位与 Shader 的坐标边界均为相对中心 (0, 1, 0) 各轴 ±2 个 AvatarRootLocal 单位；Shader 单独展示其编码边界。Contact 直接定位的米制坐标与单接收器覆盖按下节的 AvatarRootMeters 契约解释。

Four-anchor tracking uses a receiver radius of 2, measurement-point offsets of ±0.35 along each coordinate axis, and approximately 0.990 between points, all in AvatarRootLocal authored units. These values equal world metres directly only when authored and runtime Avatar-root scales are both one. The SDK's 3 m world-radius limit still constrains usable root scaling. Four-anchor coverage requires every distance measurement, unsaturated values and the reconstruction bounds; coverage displays show the common usable region of all four Receivers. Four-anchor tracking and Shader coordinates allow ±2 AvatarRootLocal units per axis relative to (0, 1, 0), with Shader encoding bounds shown separately. Contact direct tracking's metre-based coordinates and single-Receiver coverage follow the AvatarRootMeters contract below.

原生 Shader 图块把定位图案移到数据区外的边缘。冗余边缘用于检测小幅截取偏移，CRC 用于拒绝损坏的数据；不根据上一帧猜测或填补坐标。边缘定位不能修复渲染延迟、跨帧混合或冻结，仍须通过完整包校验与帧变化检查。诊断 PNG 保留独立的实验编码，不作为真实客户端捕获证据。

The native Shader tile places alignment patterns along the edges outside its payload. Redundant edges detect small capture offsets, and CRC rejects damaged data; coordinates are never guessed or filled from an earlier frame. Alignment does not repair render delay, mixed frames or freezes, so complete-packet validation and changing-frame checks remain required. Diagnostic PNGs retain their separate lab encoding and do not establish real-client capture.

## Native Contact reports / 原生 Contact 报告

SDK 3.10.6-beta.2 的高级 Proximity 报告作为独立方案接入，显示名称为 **Contact 直接定位（SDK Beta）**；原固定 SPS 方案显示为**四点定位**，保留 `Sensa/SPS/<id>/{P0,P1,P2,P3,Sample}` 与全部解算条件。Automatic SPS Setup 默认选择四点定位，Contact 方式明确选择且要求 SDK 能力可用。每个作者使用独立碰撞通道、一个跟随所选自身根点的球形 Sender 和一个 Avatar 根零偏移 Receiver，可使用明确的 SPS、Sensa 或原生 Contact 根点。

高级报告保留发送形状中点相对接收形状中点的米制位移，按接收形状轴旋转；Sensa 布置将其命名为 **AvatarRootMeters**，与按制作尺度解释的 AvatarRootLocal 分别处理。接触点相对速度保留 SDK 的角运动项；ProximitySpeed 单位为接近值／秒。公开路径为 `Sensa/Contact/<id>/` 下十个原生报告字段，加上实际世界半径 Radius 和活动 Sample，共十二字段。Sample 约每 50 ms 循环 n/32，变化年龄上限 1000 ms，停用为零；Radius 随 ScaleFactor 更新并保留 3 m 半径门控。原生报告未提供时钟、序号或稳定 Sender 标识。SensaPoint 对位置与速度共用现有校准、启用和失效停止；多点姿态留待后续。[Beta 依据与操作](../contact-sdk.md)

The advanced Proximity reports in SDK 3.10.6-beta.2 form a separate route named **Contact direct tracking (SDK Beta)**. The fixed SPS route is displayed as **Four-anchor tracking** and retains `Sensa/SPS/<id>/{P0,P1,P2,P3,Sample}` and its complete reconstruction rules. Automatic SPS Setup defaults to four-anchor tracking; Contact is explicitly selected and requires the SDK capability. Each author uses a unique collision channel, one spherical Sender following its selected own root, and one zero-offset Avatar-root Receiver. Explicit SPS, Sensa and native Contact roots are supported.

Native position retains metre-based Sender-shape-midpoint displacement relative to the Receiver-shape midpoint, rotated into Receiver shape axes. Sensa calls this frame **AvatarRootMeters**, distinct from authored-unit AvatarRootLocal. Relative contact-point velocity retains the SDK's angular-motion terms; ProximitySpeed uses proximity units per second. `Sensa/Contact/<id>/` publishes ten native report fields plus actual world Radius and activity Sample, twelve fields in total. Sample cycles through n/32 nominally every 50 ms, has a maximum 1000 ms change age and is zero while inactive. Radius follows ScaleFactor with the 3 m guard. Native reports have no clock, sequence or stable Sender identifier. Position and velocity share SensaPoint calibration, activation and invalidity stops; multi-point orientation remains future work.
